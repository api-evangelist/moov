# Moov

> Moov is the payments platform powering choice. Whether you're accepting, storing, sending, or spending money, Moov gives technical teams the APIs, SDKs, and prebuilt UI components to embed modern payments into any product. Built on direct rails to the card networks, ACH, RTP, and FedNow, Moov delivers a unified money-movement experience without the legacy stack.

## Platform overview

- [Moov platform](https://moov.io/platform): One interface to transfer funds between any two sources, using any payment options
- [Feature list](https://moov.io/platform/features): An exhaustive list of everything the Moov platform can do—growing daily
- [Product roadmap](https://moov.io/platform/roadmap): Moov's tactical plan to continuously deliver new ways to build payment experiences
